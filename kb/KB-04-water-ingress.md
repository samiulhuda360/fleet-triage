## Symptoms
- A collar that went funny for a day or two then died, often after rain or a trough or river crossing.
- Odd temperature readings, signal dropping out, then nothing.

## Cause
Water gets past a damaged seal. Internal temperature readings rise and jump about, the radio loses power, and
the electronics fail within one to three days.

## What the data shows
- Collar temperature several degrees above other collars on the same farm at the same hour, with spikes.
- Signal strength well below the collar's own normal level at the same time.
- Then the collar stops reporting while the tower and the rest of the herd are fine.

## What to tell the farmer
The collar has taken on water and needs to come off the animal and be returned. It cannot be fixed on the farm.

## Escalation
Escalate for a return and replacement (hardware fault). Quote the collar id and the last report time.
