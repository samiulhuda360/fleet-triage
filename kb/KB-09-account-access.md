title: Signing in to the farm app and adding staff
category: account_access
escalate: false
---
## Symptoms
- Cannot log in, password reset email not arriving, a new farm worker cannot see the herd.

## Cause
Usually a reset link that expired, an email typed differently from the account, or a staff member not yet
invited to the farm.

## What the data shows
- Nothing on the device side; collars are reporting normally.

## What to tell the farmer
Use Forgot password and check the spam folder for the reset link; it lasts one hour. The farm owner adds staff
under Settings, then People, then Invite.

## Escalation
No escalation needed. Escalate to the accounts team only if the owner has lost access to the email on file.
