## Symptoms
- A collar found in the paddock, hanging loose, rubbing, or twisted so the panel faces down.
- This collar keeps showing in one spot when the animal has moved.

## Cause
Straps stretch and animals lose condition, so collars loosen. A collar lying in the grass still reports, but
from where it fell.

## What the data shows
- The collar is healthy: battery, signal and GPS normal. Solar charge can be lower because the panel is shaded
  or upside down.

## What to tell the farmer
Refit the collar with the fitting guide: two fingers between strap and neck, panel on top. A replacement strap
can be ordered through the farm app.

## Escalation
No escalation needed unless the collar itself is damaged.
