## Symptoms
- Questions about the monthly invoice, the number of collars billed, or changing plan.

## Cause
Billing follows the number of active collars on the last day of the month.

## What the data shows
- Nothing on the device side.

## What to tell the farmer
Explain how the collar count is worked out and where to find invoices (Settings, then Billing). Support staff
cannot change prices or issue credits.

## Escalation
Escalate to the accounts team for disputes, double charges, refunds or cancellations. Never promise a credit.
