## Symptoms
- Too many messages, low battery texts all day, the manager should get the alerts instead.

## Cause
Default notifications are on for every collar. Farmers can tune them.

## What the data shows
- Nothing unusual on the device side.

## What to tell the farmer
Notifications are under Settings, then Alerts. Each alert type can go to the app, a text or an email, and to
different people. Low-battery alerts can be set to a daily digest.

## Escalation
No escalation needed.
