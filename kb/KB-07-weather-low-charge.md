## Symptoms
- Lots of collars on a farm showing lower battery after several cloudy or stormy days.
- Low-battery notifications across the herd at once, while collars still report.

## Cause
Collars charge from a small solar panel. Days of heavy cloud mean less charge, so every collar in the area
drops together. They recover after one or two sunny days.

## What the data shows
- Solar charge down across the whole farm or region at the same time (shared weather, not one collar).
- Night drain normal, so the batteries themselves are healthy.
- Collars still reporting.

## What to tell the farmer
Nothing is wrong with the collars. Charge recovers with the sun. If a tower on the farm has also gone quiet,
see KB-01.

## Escalation
No escalation needed unless collars stop reporting.
