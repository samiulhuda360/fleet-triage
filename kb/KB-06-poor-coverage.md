## Symptoms
- Collars in gullies or behind ridges dropping in and out, positions arriving late or in bursts.
- Signal bars low on hill blocks, but collars keep working over the day.

## Cause
Hills block the radio path to the tower. Weak and variable signal is normal on steep country and does not damage
the collars or flatten their batteries.

## What the data shows
- Signal strength low and noisy for the whole group, consistently since install.
- Battery, GPS and temperature normal; the collars always come back.
- No change over time, which is what separates it from a fault.

## What to tell the farmer
This is a coverage limitation, not a broken collar. Offer a coverage survey if they want better reception on
the worst blocks; a second tower or a repeater is a sales conversation, not a support promise.
