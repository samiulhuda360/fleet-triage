## Symptoms
- How do I add the new collars, how do I move a mob to the run-off block, do I need a new tower.

## Cause
How-to question; nothing is broken.

## What the data shows
- Nothing unusual on the device side.

## What to tell the farmer
New collars pair in the farm app under Herd, then Add collars, by scanning the QR code on each collar. Moving a
mob needs no change to towers if the new paddock is within range of an existing tower on the farm.

## Escalation
No escalation needed.
