---
id: KB-02
title: Collar battery fading (cell degradation)
category: battery_fade
escalate: true
---
## Symptoms
- One or a few collars that won't hold charge, go flat overnight, or come back every morning and drop out at
  night.
- Battery warnings on the same collar day after day, even in good weather.

## Cause
The lithium cell has lost capacity. The same overnight load now takes a bigger share of what the cell can hold,
so the battery percentage falls faster at night and the collar browns out before dawn.

## What the data shows
- Night-time drain (percent per hour when the panel produces nothing) well above the collar's own normal level,
  and above 0.5 %/h.
- Solar charge in the day is normal, which rules out weather or a dirty panel.
- Firmware has not changed recently (a firmware change points to KB-03 instead).

## What to tell the farmer
The collar's battery cell is wearing out. It is a hardware fault, not something on the farm.

## Escalation
Escalate for a replacement collar or battery module. Quote the collar id and the measured night drain.
