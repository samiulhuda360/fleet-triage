## Symptoms
- An animal showing on the road, in the neighbour's paddock, or jumping around while it is standing still.
- One collar whose track looks like a scribble compared with the rest of the mob.

## Cause
The collar's GPS module is degrading. Position accuracy (HDOP) gets steadily worse over days and some fixes fail.

## What the data shows
- HDOP rising over several days and above 2.8, against a normal level of 1 to 1.6.
- A slow fall in fix rate on that collar only.
- Battery, signal and temperature normal.

## What to tell the farmer
The collar's GPS is wearing out, so its positions are unreliable. The animal itself is very likely where the rest
of the mob is.

## Escalation
Escalate for a replacement collar. Quote the collar id and the HDOP trend.
